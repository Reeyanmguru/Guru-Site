How the matching score works

Each possible teacher → learner connection is indexed by normalized skill. We score viable pairs across the full demo directory, then rank circles for the trait you searched:

  • 45% mutual skill coverage: how well each person can teach what the other wants to learn.
  • 30% searched-trait fit: whether the circle directly teaches and wants to learn your chosen skill.
  • 20% reciprocity: a strong boost when both people give and receive.
  • 5% dimension fit: a small bonus when both profiles share the selected dimension.

Exact and curated synonym matches count most. A one-way fit can still appear, clearly labeled. The index avoids comparing unrelated profile pairs, keeping the search efficient as the directory grows.

These are illustrative profiles. Saved circles are local to this browser; shared matching requires the connected account and profile backend.
